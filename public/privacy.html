♠ Poker Night

Your Poker Night account

Updated 11 November 2026. Poker Night uses play chips. There are no deposits, withdrawals of money, or cash prizes.

Google sign-in

Google verifies your identity. We store your Google account identifier and display name so you can return to the same Poker Night wallet. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar.

Game and wallet records

We store wallet balances, Cash buy-ins, cash-outs and completed-hand settlements in our Neon database. Gameplay records support hand history, statistics and bot evaluation. Bots use public opponent information; human hidden cards are excluded from the bot-training archive. Your chosen table name and avatar are visible to others at that table.

Cookies and storage

An account-session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. A separate token protects sign-in requests. Browser storage remembers appearance, audio and table-session preferences. Google also processes the sign-in interaction under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can use Custom and Tournament without Google sign-in. Cash requires an account. Cash out before signing out. For account-data questions or a deletion request, contact conantai2006@gmail.com. Account deletion requests require verification of ownership; settled records involving other players may need to be retained in de-identified form to preserve game and wallet integrity.
